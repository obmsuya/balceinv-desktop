# Invoices, quotations and delivery notes

A guide for the Balce sales and support team. No technical knowledge needed. Button names are written as **English (Kiswahili)**, as the app shows them. The full feature explanation is in [Invoices](../features/invoices.md).

**The short answer:**

- **Invoices (Ankara)** in the sidebar makes branded invoices, proforma invoices, quotations and delivery notes, and lets you bill a supplier.
- Each one becomes a PDF the shop can share on WhatsApp or print.
- Invoices track what is still owed. Stock only goes out when you press **Mark delivered**.

---

## 1. Switch it on (once)

1. **Settings (Mipangilio)** → **Features**: switch on **Customers**, plus **Suppliers** if they will bill suppliers.
2. Switch on **Invoices (Ankara)**.
3. **Settings** → **Business** → the **Invoices** card. Fill in:

| Field | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Business email** | Printed in the header of every document. |
| **How customers pay you** | Bank name and account, Lipa number. Printed under the totals of invoices and proforma invoices, so the customer knows where to pay. One payment method per line. |
| **Default terms** | For example "Payment within 30 days". Filled in on every new invoice; can be changed per invoice. |
| **Days to pay** | Sets the due date automatically (issue date + days). 30 if left alone. |

The logo and brand colour come from **Settings → Branding**, the same as receipts.

## 2. Make an invoice

1. **Invoices (Ankara)** → **New (Mpya)** → **Invoice (Ankara)**.
2. **Bill to (Kwa):** choose the customer, or add a new one there.
3. Check the dates. Type their order number (LPO) in **Their order no.** if they gave one.
4. **Add the items:**
   - Search a product: its name, unit and price fill in by themselves. The price can still be changed.
   - **Add typed line (Ongeza mstari wa kuandika)** is for things that are not products: transport, labour, installation. Write what it is and its price.
   - **Discount (Punguzo)** on a line takes money off that line only.
5. **Save draft (Hifadhi rasimu).** The preview on the right shows the exact PDF the customer will receive.
6. When it is right, press **Save and send (Hifadhi na tuma)**. A sent invoice cannot be edited any more. To change it, cancel it and make a new one.

## 3. Send it

Open the invoice and press:

- **Share (Tuma):** on a phone, WhatsApp, email and other apps open with the PDF attached. On a computer, the PDF is saved; send it from the file.
- **PDF:** saves the file to print or attach.

## 4. Get paid and deliver

These can happen in any order.

- **Record payment (Rekodi malipo):**
  - Enter the amount (the full balance is filled in) and choose cash, card or mobile money. Add the M-Pesa or bank reference if there is one.
  - The status changes to **Part paid** or **Paid**.
- **Mark delivered:**
  - Press it when the goods actually leave. The products go out of the shop's stock, and the customer now owes the invoice.
  - For typed lines only (work, services), press it when the work is done.

> If the customer pays **before** delivery, their customer page shows **In credit**. That is normal: the money is waiting for the goods.

**Overdue (Imechelewa)** shows in red when the due date has passed and something is still owed. Follow these up first; the **Overdue** card on the Invoices page totals them.

## 5. Quotations and proforma invoices

1. **New** → **Quotation (Nukuu ya bei)** or **Proforma invoice (Ankara ya awali)**. The date field is **Valid until**.
2. Send it the same way.
3. When the customer agrees, open it and press **Make invoice**. A new draft invoice is made with the same items, and the quotation shows **Accepted**.

Quotations and proforma invoices never move stock or money.

## 6. Delivery notes

- From a sent invoice, press **Make delivery note (Hati ya kupokelea)**. It has the same items with no prices, and shows which invoice it belongs to.
- Print it for the driver. The customer signs **Received by**.
- Or make one directly: **New → Delivery note**.

## 7. Bill a supplier

Use this when a supplier owes the shop: damaged or missing goods, a charge, or goods the shop sold to them.

1. Open the supplier → **Bill this supplier (Mtoze msambazaji huyu)**. Or go to **Invoices → New → Supplier invoice**.
2. Add the items, then **Save and send**.
3. Press **Charge the supplier (Mtoze msambazaji)**. What you owe them goes down; any products on it leave stock.
4. **Record payment** and choose how it was settled:
   - **Taken off what we owe:** the supplier keeps it off their next bill. No money moves.
   - **Cash, Bank or Mobile money:** the supplier paid you.

The supplier's statement shows the invoice and any money they paid.

## 8. Purchase orders for suppliers

**Suppliers → the supplier → Orders → open the order → PDF or Share.** The purchase order lists the items and the expected prices, with your logo. It asks the supplier to quote the PO number on their invoice. When the goods come, receive them with **Receive stock (Pokea stoku)** as before.

## Common questions

**I made a mistake on a sent invoice.**
Cancel it and make a new one. A cancelled invoice cannot have payments on it; cancel the payments first (open the payment → **Cancel this payment**). If it was already delivered, cancelling brings the stock back.

**The customer paid by mistake on the wrong invoice.**
Cancel that payment on the invoice, then record it on the right one.

**Who can make invoices?**
The owner, and anyone whose role has the **Invoices** permissions (**Roles**):
- **View** sees them.
- **Create** makes them.
- **Edit** sends, delivers and takes payments.
- **Delete** cancels.

Staff only see invoices for the shops they work in.

**The invoice total is not in today's sales on the dashboard.**
Correct: the dashboard and sales reports count till sales only. Invoice money is on the Invoices page and, if the books are on, in Profit & loss.

**Can I send it in Kiswahili?**
Yes. The document follows the language of whoever downloads it: Kiswahili gives "Ankara", "Kiasi kinachodaiwa", and the amount in words in Kiswahili.
