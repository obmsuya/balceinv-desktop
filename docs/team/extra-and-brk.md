# Extra and Brk lines

## Short answer

- **Extra (Ziada)** raises the price of one cart line. Every cashier has it, next to **Discount (Punguzo)**.
- **Brk lines** record something sold through the shop that is not the shop's stock, for example an item fetched from another shop. The owner turns them on in **Settings → Features (Vipengele)**.
- **Reports → Brk and Extra (Brk na Ziada)** lists every Extra and Brk line. Use it for the evening count and to pay whoever is owed.

"Brk" is short for brokerage. The client chose it so customers do not understand the word. It used to be called "winga".

Tap the small **?** next to **Add Brk line** or **Brk share** for a short explanation on the screen. The Extra box explains itself at the top.

## Raise a price with Extra

1. Add the product to the cart.
2. Press **Extra (Ziada)** on that line.
3. Choose **TZS** and type the amount to add to **each** item, or choose **Percent (Asilimia)**.
4. Press **Apply (Weka)**. The line and the total go up.

To take it off, press the badge again and press **Remove (Ondoa)**.

## Sell a Brk line

1. The owner turns on **Settings → Features (Vipengele) → Brk lines (Mistari ya Brk)** and saves.
2. At the till, press **Add Brk line (Ongeza mstari wa Brk)** under the cart.
3. Type what was sold, how many, and the price each.
4. Type the **Brk share**: the part paid out, to the broker or the other shop. Leave it empty if the shop keeps it all.
5. Press **Add to cart (Weka kwenye kikapu)**, then take payment as usual.

The same button is on **New order** for customer orders.

## See Extra and Brk in reports

Go to **Reports (Ripoti)** and pick the period, for example **Today (Leo)** for the evening count.

- **Brk and Extra (Brk na Ziada):** one row per line, with the time, receipt, staff member, item, Extra, Brk and Brk share, and totals at the top.
- **Sales per day (Mauzo kwa siku)** and **Sales per staff member (Mauzo kwa mfanyakazi)** get Extra, Brk and Brk share columns when the period has any.
- **Sales report (Ripoti ya mauzo)** lists the totals under "Other figures".

## Common questions

- **Does the customer see the extra, the word Brk or the share?** No. The receipt shows only the item name and the price they pay.
- **Does a Brk line change stock?** No. It is not a product.
- **Where does the money show?** In sales and the cash taken, like any sale. The Brk share counts as cost, so profit shows what the shop really earned.
- **Who gets the extra?** The report shows which staff member made each sale. It does not record a fundi's name, so write it in your book if someone else brought the customer.
- **Can I refund a Brk line?** Not on its own yet. Void the sale and sell again.
- **The Add Brk line button is missing.** The owner has not turned the switch on, or the till page needs a refresh after saving.
- **Why is Discount missing but Extra is there?** Discount needs the till discount permission on the person's role. Extra does not.
