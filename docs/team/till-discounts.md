# Discounts at the till

A guide for the Balce sales and support team. No technical knowledge needed. Button names are written as **English (Kiswahili)**.

There are two kinds of discount:

| | Who sets it | Where |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Planned discount** | The owner, ahead of time (for example "10% off sugar this week") | **Discounts (Punguzo)** page. It applies by itself at the till. |
| **Cashier discount** | A cashier, on the spot, for one cart line | The **Discount (Punguzo)** button on the line at the till |

Both can apply to the same line. The cashier discount comes on top of the planned one, and a line can never go below zero.

## Giving a cashier discount

1. Add the product to the cart.
2. On that line, press **Discount (Punguzo)**.
3. Choose **Percent (Asilimia)** or the currency (TZS), type the amount, and press **Apply (Weka)**. The box shows how much it takes off.
4. The line shows the discount in green (for example **−10%**). To change or remove it, press it again; **Remove (Ondoa)** takes it off.

The receipt shows **Cashier discount (Punguzo la keshia)** on its own line, with the name of the cashier who served.

## Who can give one

- The **owner** always can.
- Other staff need **Give discounts at the till (Kutoa punguzo kaunta)**. The owner ticks it in **Roles (Majukumu)** → the role → **Manage permissions**. No role has it until the owner ticks it.
- Staff without it do not see the button.
- There is **no limit**. Anyone allowed can take any amount off, up to the full price (100%). Who is allowed is the owner's decision, through Roles.

## Common questions

**"The Discount button is missing."** The user's role does not have **Give discounts at the till**. Ask the owner to tick it in Roles.

**"Did the old app have this?"** Yes. The old till had a "% off" box anyone could use. It was removed on 29 Sep 2026 because any cashier could sell at any price with no record, and it came back in version 2.0.4 behind the permission, with every cashier discount on the receipt.
