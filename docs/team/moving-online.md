# Desktop or online, and moving a shop online

A guide for the Balce sales and support team. No technical knowledge needed. Button names are written as **English (Kiswahili)**.

---

## Does a shop's data follow them between the desktop app and pos.faltasi.com?

**No.** The desktop app and the online version are two separate systems:

- **Desktop app:** everything (products, sales, stock, users) is saved **on that one computer**.
- **Online (pos.faltasi.com):** everything is saved **on our online server**.

Signing up online with the same email does **not** show the desktop business. It creates a new, empty one. The two never sync.

What does follow the shop:

| | Where the data can be used |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Online** | Any phone, tablet or computer with internet. The owner can check the shop from home or while travelling. |
| **Desktop** | Other tills on the same shop network connect to the main computer and share its data. The online backup (Settings → Backups) can bring the business back onto another computer if the old one breaks. |

**Which should a shop use?**
- **Online:** the owner wants to see the shop from anywhere, or has more than one branch.
- **Desktop:** the internet is weak and the shop must keep selling when it drops.

A shop should use **one** of the two. Using both means two separate sets of records.

---

## Moving a shop from the desktop app to online

Use this when a shop on the desktop app wants to go online. **Everything moves:**
- products, variants and stock;
- sales and receipts;
- customers and what they owe;
- suppliers and purchases;
- orders and discounts;
- the books;
- users and roles;
- the logo, product photos and receipt photos.

Nobody types anything again, and everyone signs in online with the **same email and password** they use on the computer.

**Before you start:** the desktop app must be **version 2.0.2 or newer**. The owner must do step 1, because only the owner sees the moving card.

### 1. On the shop's computer

1. Open **Settings (Mipangilio)** → **Backups (Nakala rudufu)**.
2. Find **Move this business online (Hamishia biashara hii mtandaoni)**.
3. Press **Save the moving file (Hifadhi faili la kuhamia)** and save it somewhere easy to reach: a USB stick, or the Desktop so it can be emailed. The file ends in `.balce`.

### 2. Online

1. On any computer, open **pos.faltasi.com**. On a computer that is already signed in, sign out first.
2. On the first screen, find **Already use Balce on a computer? (Tayari unatumia Balce kwenye kompyuta?)** and press **Move my business here (Hamishia biashara yangu hapa)**.
3. Press **Choose the moving file (Chagua faili la kuhamia)** and pick the `.balce` file.
4. Press **Move my business (Hamisha biashara yangu)**. Keep the page open; it takes up to a minute for a big shop.
5. When it says **… is online (… iko mtandaoni)**, press **Sign in (Ingia)**. Sign in with the owner's email and the **same password** as on the computer.

### 3. After moving

- Check a few products, today's sales and the stock levels together with the owner.
- **From now on, the shop sells online only.** The computer keeps its old copy but does not see anything done online. Staff should stop using the desktop app to sell.
- The online business starts a **14-day free trial**. Days the shop paid on the desktop do **not** move automatically. If they had paid days left, activate their new **Subscription ID** with the sales tool for the remaining days (see [The sales tool](sales-tool.md)).

### When it does not work

| The screen says… | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **This business is already online (Biashara hii tayari iko mtandaoni)** | It was moved before. Sign in instead. |
| **One of this business's users already has an online account with the same email** | Someone in this shop already signed up online with that email. Change that user's email on the computer (Users), save a new moving file, and try again. |
| **This is not a Balce moving file** | The wrong file was chosen, or it was damaged while copying. Save a new moving file on the computer. |
| **This file comes from a newer Balce** | The desktop app is newer than the online server. Wait a day for the online update, then try again. |
| **The moving file is too large to upload** | The file is over 64 MB (usually many large product photos). Send it to the technical team. |
