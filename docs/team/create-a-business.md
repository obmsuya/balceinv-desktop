# Creating a business on Balce

A guide for the Balce sales and support team. No technical knowledge needed.

Every shop needs a **business account** before it can sell. There are three ways to make one:

| Situation | Use |
| :--- | :--- |
| You are at the shop and it will use the **app on its own computer** | [A. At the shop, with the desktop app](#a-at-the-shop-with-the-desktop-app) |
| You are at the shop and it will work **online, in the browser** | [B. At the shop, online](#b-at-the-shop-online) |
| The customer wants to **do it alone**, from home or on the phone | [C. The customer does it alone](#c-the-customer-does-it-alone) |

> **Which one should the shop use?**
> - **Desktop app:** works without internet, keeps everything on the shop's computer, and other tills in the shop can connect to it. Best for shops with weak internet.
> - **Online (pos.faltasi.com):** nothing to install, works on any computer, tablet or phone with internet, and the owner can check the shop from anywhere.

Before you start, have these ready with the owner:

- The **business name** (for example *Duka la Amina*)
- The **type of business** (pharmacy, hardware store, supermarket…)
- The owner's **full name** and **email address**
- A **password** the owner will remember (at least 8 letters or numbers)
- Optional: phone number, address and TIN number

The app can be in English or Kiswahili. The **language button** is at the top right of every screen. The button names in this guide are written as **English (Kiswahili)**.

---

## A. At the shop, with the desktop app

### 1. Install the app

1. On the shop's computer, open the Balce download page (the **Downloads** table in the main README).
2. Download the installer for the computer (Windows, Mac or Linux).
3. Open the downloaded file and follow the installer. If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
4. Open **Balce** from the desktop or the Start menu.

### 2. If the shop used the old Balce before

If the old Balce was on this computer, the first screen shows a box saying the old data is on this computer.

1. Click **Bring over my old data (Hamisha data zangu za zamani)**.
2. Check the business name, the owner, and the numbers of products, staff and sales shown.
3. Click **Bring over my data**. Wait until it finishes. The old file is never changed.
4. Everyone signs in with their **old email and old password**. If the screen shows temporary passwords, write them down and give each person theirs.

You are done. Skip to [After the business is created](#after-the-business-is-created).

### 3. Create a new business

1. The app opens on **Your business (Biashara yako)**.
2. Type the **Business name**.
3. Click **Business type** and choose the shop's type. This matters: it decides which list of common products the shop sees later.
4. Fill in phone, address and TIN if the owner has them. They can be added later.
5. Click **Continue to owner account (Endelea kwenye akaunti ya mmiliki)**.
6. Type the owner's **full name**, **email address** and **password**.
7. Click **Create business account (Unda akaunti ya biashara)**.

The app signs the owner in and opens the till. The desktop app starts with a **14-day free trial**; the owner pays for a plan inside the app before it ends.

---

## B. At the shop, online

1. On the shop's computer, tablet or phone, open the browser (Chrome is best).
2. Go to **pos.faltasi.com**.
3. The first time on that device, the **Your business (Biashara yako)** page opens by itself. If you see **Sign in (Ingia)** instead, click **Set up your business (Weka biashara yako)** under the sign in button.
4. Follow steps 2 to 7 of [Create a new business](#3-create-a-new-business) above.

The owner is signed in and the till opens. Tip: on a phone, open the browser menu and choose **Add to Home screen**, so the shop can open Balce like an app.

## C. The customer does it alone

Send the customer this message (WhatsApp or SMS):

> Karibu Balce! Fungua **pos.faltasi.com** kwenye simu au kompyuta.
> 1. Jaza jina la biashara na uchague aina ya biashara yako.
> 2. Bonyeza **Endelea kwenye akaunti ya mmiliki**.
> 3. Jaza jina lako, barua pepe na nenosiri (angalau herufi 8).
> 4. Bonyeza **Unda akaunti ya biashara**.
> Ukimaliza utaingia moja kwa moja kwenye duka lako. Ukihitaji msaada, bonyeza **Msaada** chini ya ukurasa.

In English:

> Welcome to Balce! Open **pos.faltasi.com** on your phone or computer.
> 1. Fill in your business name and choose your type of business.
> 2. Press **Continue to owner account**.
> 3. Fill in your name, email and a password (at least 8 characters).
> 4. Press **Create business account**.
> You will go straight into your shop. If you need help, press **Support** at the bottom of the page.

## After the business is created

Help the owner with these first steps:

1. **Add products:** go to **Products (Bidhaa) → Add product (Ongeza bidhaa)**. At the top, **Pick from common products (Chagua kutoka bidhaa za kawaida)** lets them search the ready list for their business type, and the name, unit, category and price fill in by themselves. If the list is empty, see [Loading common products](common-products.md).
2. **Add staff:** go to **Users (Watumiaji) → Add User (Ongeza mtumiaji)** and add each cashier with their own email and password.
3. **Switch on extras:** in **Settings (Mipangilio) → Features (Vipengele)**, turn on only what the shop needs: suppliers, customers and credit (madeni), orders, money and books.

## When something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| "This email is already in use" | That email already has a Balce account. Use another email, or click **Sign in (Ingia)** and sign in with it. |
| "Too many new businesses from this network" | There were ten tries to create a business from this internet connection in the last hour. Wait one hour, or use another connection (for example mobile data). |
| The page does not open | Check the internet. If only pos.faltasi.com fails, wait 30 minutes and try again, or try on mobile data. |
| A cashier forgot the password | The owner opens **Users (Watumiaji)**, edits that person and sets a new password. |
| The owner forgot the password | Contact the Balce technical team. (The **Forgot password?** link on the sign in page does not work yet.) |
