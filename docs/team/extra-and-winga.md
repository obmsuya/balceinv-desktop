# Extra and winga lines

## Short answer

- **Extra (Ziada)** raises the price of one cart line. Every cashier has it, next to **Discount (Punguzo)**.
- **Winga lines** record something a winga sold through the shop. The owner turns them on in **Settings → Features (Vipengele)**.

## Raise a price with Extra

1. Add the product to the cart.
2. Press **Extra (Ziada)** on that line.
3. Choose **TZS** and type the amount to add to **each** item, or choose **Percent (Asilimia)**.
4. Press **Apply (Weka)**. The line and the total go up.

To take it off, press the badge again and press **Remove (Ondoa)**.

## Sell for a winga

1. The owner turns on **Settings → Features (Vipengele) → Winga lines (Mistari ya winga)** and saves.
2. At the till, press **Add winga line (Ongeza mstari wa winga)** under the cart.
3. Type what was sold, how many, and the price each.
4. Type the **winga share**: what the winga takes. Leave it empty if the shop keeps it all.
5. Press **Add to cart (Weka kwenye kikapu)**, then take payment as usual.

The same button is on **New order** for customer orders.

## Common questions

- **Does the customer see the extra or the winga share?** No. The receipt shows only the price they pay.
- **Does a winga line change stock?** No. It is not a product.
- **Where does the money show?** In sales and the cash taken, like any sale. The winga share counts as cost, so profit shows what the shop really earned.
- **Can I refund a winga line?** Not on its own yet. Void the sale and sell again.
- **The Add winga line button is missing.** The owner has not turned the switch on, or the till page needs a refresh after saving.
- **Why is Discount missing but Extra is there?** Discount needs the till discount permission on the person's role. Extra does not.
