# Recording expenses (money out)

A guide for the Balce sales and support team. No technical knowledge needed.

Balce writes down every **sale, stock change and customer or supplier payment** by itself. The shop only needs to record money that goes out for other things: rent, salaries, LUKU, transport and so on. This happens in **Money (Fedha)**, which appears in the menu once the books are switched on.

Button names are written as **English (Kiswahili)**.

---

## 1. Switch the books on (once, by the owner)

1. Open **Settings (Mipangilio)** → **Features (Vipengele)**.
2. Under **Accounting (Uhasibu)** choose:
   - **Simple books (Hesabu rahisi):** for most shops. Record money out, money the owner puts in or takes out, and see profit and what the shop owns and owes.
   - **Full accounting (Uhasibu kamili):** for shops with an accountant. Adds the chart of accounts, manual entries, month closing and the trial balance.
3. Save. **Money (Fedha)** now appears in the menu.

## 2. Start the books (once)

The first time anyone opens **Money (Fedha)**, it asks **Start your books (Anzisha vitabu vya hesabu)**:

1. Choose where the books start:
   - **Start from my existing records (Anza na rekodi nilizonazo):** recommended when the shop has already been selling in Balce. Earlier sales and stock are used.
   - **Start from today (Anza kuanzia leo):** only what happens from today counts.
2. Enter how much money the shop has now: **Cash in the drawer**, **Mobile money balance** and **Bank balance**. This is optional; it can be added later with **Owner put in**.
3. Press **Start the books (Anzisha vitabu)**.

Only the owner, or someone whose role may edit accounting, can do this. Everyone else sees a message asking the owner to start the books.

## 3. Record an expense

1. Open **Money (Fedha)**.
2. Press **Money out (Pesa iliyotoka)**.
3. Fill in:
   - **What was it for? (Ilikuwa ya nini?)**: Rent, Salaries, Electricity & water (LUKU), Transport, Phone & internet, Bank & mobile money charges, Repairs, Licences & levies, Shop supplies, Marketing, or Other expenses.
   - **Amount**
   - **Paid from (Imelipwa kutoka)**: cash, mobile money or bank.
   - **Receipt photo (Picha ya risiti)**: optional, but good to keep.
   - If the shop is VAT registered and the receipt shows VAT, tick the VAT box and add the supplier's TIN and the EFD receipt number.
4. Press **Save (Hifadhi)**.

The expense appears at the top of the table on the Money page, under **Money out**.

## Other things recorded on the same page

Press **Record other (Rekodi nyingine)** for:

| Choice | Use it when |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Other money in** | Money came in that is not from selling, such as rent the shop receives. |
| **Move money** | Cash taken to the bank, mobile money withdrawn to cash, and so on. |
| **Owner put money in** | The owner added money to the business. |
| **Owner took money out** | The owner took money for personal use. This is **not** a shop expense. |

## Mistakes

Open the record in the table and press **Reverse (Batilisha)**, giving a reason. The record stays, marked as reversed, so there is always a trail.

## Who can record

The role needs the **accounting** permission. Give it in **Roles (Majukumu)** to the person who pays the bills, for example the manager.

## Where to see the results

Everything recorded here feeds the reports in **Reports (Ripoti)**: profit and loss, the money summary, the cash book, and (with full accounting) the balance sheet, trial balance and VAT return.
